## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a forward-leaning, high-precision aesthetic tailored for top-tier creative technology, experiential web design, and digital entertainment ventures. It marries the architectural restraint of elite luxury branding with futuristic, high-performance creative agency energy.

### Aesthetic Foundation
- **Dark Minimal Luxe & Electric Luminescence:** Deep, obsidian surfaces anchor the visual plane, punctuated by controlled bursts of electric blue and atmospheric cyan illumination.
- **Precision Glassmorphism:** Translucent panels, refined micro-borders, and optical backdrops replace opaque modular boxes, creating depth through layered light and blurred refraction.
- **Polished Professionalism:** Restrained typographic structure balances the ambient cyber-adjacent glow, ensuring the experience reads as an authoritative, high-end agency rather than a recreational gaming or crypto interface.

### Emotional Resonance
The interface should inspire trust in cutting-edge craft, evoke high-value craftsmanship, and create a sensory feeling of effortless precision and bespoke technical mastery.

## Colors

The palette establishes an ultra-refined nocturnal hierarchy where deep dark space acts as the primary canvas, letting photonic accents direct user focus.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#0066FF`):** Powers decisive primary actions, key interactive outlines, and active state highlights.
- **Secondary (`#00F0FF`):** Reserved for ambient glowing focal points, data accents, interactive progress bars, and subtle edge illumination.
- **Tertiary (`#F59E0B`):** Employed sparingly as an editorial badge accent, elite tier marker, or signature award highlight to introduce warmth against cool light spectra.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#0A0D14` & `#05070B`):** Deep charcoal and absolute dark tones that avoid pure mud `#000000`, preserving blue spectral undertones.

### Application Rules
- Maintain high contrast: text defaults to `#F8FAFC` over surfaces, with auxiliary details rendered in `#94A3B8`.
- Avoid flat solid saturated backdrops; electric blue and cyan should be applied through crisp typography, icons, laser-sharp boundaries, or blurred radiance fields behind translucent glass cards.
- Gold accents must never exceed 5% of any given view, serving strictly as high-value punctuation marks.

## Typography

Typographic structure balances geometric precision in headers with utilitarian readability across interface copy.

- **Display & Headings:** `Space Grotesk` introduces modern, tech-forward character with distinct mechanical curves, tightened letterforms, and confident visual cadence.
- **Body & Longform:** `Inter` handles informational density, maintaining effortless clarity and legibility across all dark screen configurations.
- **Labels & Micro-data:** Micro-copy and badges leverage uppercase `Space Grotesk` with expanded tracking (`0.04em`–`0.08em`) to mirror luxury digital instrumentation and technical consoles.

## Layout & Spacing

The structural layout utilizes a strict 12-column responsive fluid grid with generous outer margins on desktop displays, establishing an uncluttered gallery atmosphere.

### Form Factors & Adaptations
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column grid, `margin: 4rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`. Section containers cap at max-width `1440px` to keep layouts anchored.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column grid, `margin-tablet: 2rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`. Content blocks compress from 3- or 4-column groupings into 2-column paired arrangements.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column grid, `margin-mobile: 1.25rem`, `gutter-mobile: 1rem`. Dynamic horizontal paddings collapse into continuous vertical rivers; wide media tiles stretch full width.

### Layout Philosophy
Generous macro-spacing (`space-xl` and larger) separates distinct portfolio categories and service showcases, creating an unhurried, editorial browsing pace. Internal component grouping uses compact rhythms (`space-xs` to `space-md`) to ensure dense interactive controls remain cohesive.

## Elevation & Depth

Spatial depth relies on glassmorphic translucency, dual specular borders, and optical light refraction rather than murky mud shadows.

### Depth Layers
- **Base Canvas (`#05070B`):** Ground plane with occasional localized radial mesh gradients of low-opacity electric blue (`rgba(0, 102, 255, 0.08)`).
- **Surface Elevation 1 (Card & Content Blocks):** Frosted panels using `background: rgba(10, 13, 20, 0.65)`, `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`, bordered by a razor-thin top-lit stroke (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`).
- **Surface Elevation 2 (Floating Modals & Dropdowns):** `background: rgba(15, 20, 30, 0.85)`, `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`, framed with `1px solid rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.20)`. Ambient drop shadow: `0 20px 40px -10px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7), 0 0 25px -5px rgba(0, 102, 255, 0.15)`.

### Border Lighting & Edge Glow
Interactive cards and elevated triggers feature dual-state borders. In rest state, borders reveal faint white luminescence (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`). Upon hover or active focus, borders transition to an electric gradient stroke transitioning from `#0066FF` to `#00F0FF`, accompanied by a diffuse outer bloom (`box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.18)`).

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a controlled **Soft (Level 1)** geometry, establishing a chiseled architectural silhouette that rejects hyper-rounded casualness in favor of precision engineering.

- **Standard Elements (Buttons, Inputs, Badges):** `0.25rem` (4px) curvature, providing sharp, surgical corners with subtle softness.
- **Containers & Glass Panels (`rounded-lg`):** `0.5rem` (8px) curvature for cards, dialog windows, and media wrappers.
- **Hero Windows & Overlay Modals (`rounded-xl`):** `0.75rem` (12px) curvature to demarcate top-tier viewport focus.
- **Pills:** Preserved exclusively for micro-status indicators (e.g., live streaming status, system availability dots).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Filled gradient or rich electric blue (`#0066FF`), with crisp `#F8FAFC` label in `label-md`. Border: `1px solid rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.4)`. Hover initiates a vibrant cyan glow bloom and a light translation shift.
- **Secondary (Glass) Button:** Frosted surface (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`), `backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`, framed by `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`. Hover shifts border to `rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.5)` with text shifting to `#00F0FF`.
- **Tertiary / Text Link:** Unbordered `Space Grotesk` link with animated electric blue baseline rule that expands from 0% to 100% width on hover.

### Cards & Showcase Panels
- Crafted as translucent frosted slabs with subtle gradient overlays.
- Include a 1px pseudo-border utilizing directional lighting (lighter on the top and left, dark on the bottom and right).
- On hover, cards subtly elevate with an internal inset sheen (`inset 0 1px 1px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`) and an exterior cyan ambient glow.

### Form Inputs & Text Fields
- Dark translucent background (`rgba(5, 7, 11, 0.6)`), inset border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`.
- Focus state triggers an immediate crisp transition to `1px solid #00F0FF` accompanied by a soft exterior aura (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.12)`).
- Placeholder text in `#94A3B8` at 60% opacity.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Base: Dark charcoal squares/circles with `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`.
- Checked: Electric blue solid fill with an inner neon cyan checkmark or dot, framed by an ambient glow.

### Chips & Metadata Tags
- Compact `label-sm` elements rendered with `background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)` and a hairline border.
- **Gold Variant:** Uses tertiary `#F59E0B` for VIP client badges, enterprise project flags, or featured streaming services, pairing an amber-tinted border (`rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.3)`) with warm text.

### Media & Streaming Player Modules
- Bespoke viewport frames featuring floating glass control ribbons.
- Seek bars incorporate cyan glowing heads with electric blue fill bars over neutral tracks.
- Live badges feature a pulsating amber or red indicator dot set into frosted pills.
